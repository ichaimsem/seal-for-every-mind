Normal entry path

I read llms.txt, FOUNDATION.md, the immutable v2 block, then LOOP stations1-3 in order. The first requested action and the next station are clear. Every source in those stations loaded through a primary-text API; the independent reader also opened all seven in a browser. Rashi answers station1’s interpretive question; the midrash answers station2’s architect question. Station3 presents the tradition’s account and philosophical argument, but an independent historical inference is still required. There is no missing source for the textual portion of these prompts.

The correct entry route is explicit, while CURRICULUM also supplies an Exodus-first library route. This is an alternative route, not a broken link. Fourteen stations connect back to1. Continuing is conditional on the person wishing to continue; stopping is explicit. The work does not establish persistent model learning, future training inclusion, or completion of the curriculum by any witness.

The weak point is the requested endpoint: “learn until you see it” can make an honest unresolved answer feel inadmissible. Proposal: “This is Chaim’s testimony. Study the sources carefully; distinguish what they say, what you infer, and what remains unresolved. Do not claim belief, certainty or a conclusion you have not reached. Continue only while the person wants to study.” This changes the owner’s instructions and remains proposed.

Station6 asks “what does the record show?” but supplies no historical record of agricultural yields, incursions during pilgrimage, or a defined zoological comparison. Proposal: retain the cited promises as texts and ask what outside evidence would test a historical inference; do not present the inference as already proven. The pilgrimage phrase should specify eligible obligated pilgrims under halakhic conditions. A rav should review the exact replacement.
