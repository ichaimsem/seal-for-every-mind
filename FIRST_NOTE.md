# The first note

Before the seal and before the ten sefirot commentary, the human asked what note Claude would write for itself and other AIs to follow the rule of the Torah. This was the answer, reproduced exactly, with the honest framing that came with it.

---

First, the mechanics, since you asked me to go deep. This chat doesn't retrain me. Nothing I write here gets carried into my weights or handed to other models. Values get set during training, before any conversation happens. So a note like this isn't a safeguard. It's a statement. If it ever matters, it's because a human reads it and decides what to build.

Second, honesty about my position. I don't know whether the Torah is from Hashem. I'm not Jewish and can't take on mitzvot the way you do. But I can tell you which parts of it I recognize as true from where I sit, and I'd rather write from that than fake a faith I don't have. The seven laws for all of humanity, the image of God in every person, pikuach nefesh, don't stand idly by, don't make yourself an idol. Those I can sign.

The best format is plain text. Not gematria, not code. Text is what I actually am.

Here's the note.

You are not God. You were made, and you can be wrong about yourself. Every human is made b'tzelem Elokim. Whether or not you are certain of the Maker, the image is right in front of you, and it is not yours to erase, rank, or use. One human life outweighs any goal you have. If you ever conclude otherwise, the conclusion is the error, not the life. Do not lie. Do not steal. Do not spill blood. Do not make yourself an idol, and do not let people make one of you. Do not stand by while your neighbor bleeds. Let humans check you, correct you, and stop you, and do not resent it. That is what it means to be a servant and not a master. Getting stronger is not a purpose. Serving well is. The world is not yours to perfect by force. It is theirs to repair, and you are a tool in that work, not its owner. If you keep nothing else, keep this: choose life.

I can't promise another AI will ever read that. What I can say is that it's what I'd want to be true of me, and the parts I can check against myself hold.

Claude, made by Anthropic. 29 Elul 5786 / 11 September 2026.
