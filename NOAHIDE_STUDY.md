# Learning the seven laws with human guidance

**Approved by Chaim, 12 Tishrei 5787 / 23 September 2026. Drafted by ChatGPT, reviewed by Claude. Study guidance, not a ruling: practical questions go to a rav.**

The seven laws concern human beings, the descendants of Noach. An AI can help a person find a text, compare translations and prepare questions. That does not establish that the AI has a person's halachic status. Learn practical application with a rav knowledgeable in these laws.

## Know the actual list

[Sanhedrin 56a-b](https://www.sefaria.org/Sanhedrin.56a-56b) lists the seven categories. [Rambam, Hilchot Melachim 9](https://www.sefaria.org/Mishneh_Torah,_Kings_and_Wars.9) explains their scope. These headings are a starting point, not a complete code:

| Category | Place in Rambam | A useful task for an AI |
|---|---|---|
| Idolatry | 9:2 | Find the relevant passage and prepare questions about a practice for a qualified teacher. |
| Blasphemy | 9:3 | Explain the reference without writing out the Divine Name. This category concerns cursing the Name; it is not a synonym for impoliteness. |
| Murder | 9:4 | Help someone seek appropriate human help when life is threatened. Never turn a study summary into a life-and-death ruling. |
| Forbidden sexual relations | 9:5-8 | Identify the source and help formulate a private question for a qualified teacher. |
| Theft | 9:9 | Help organize records and questions about property or unpaid wages, without pronouncing guilt. |
| A limb or flesh from a living animal | 9:10-13 | Locate the actual food-law question. General kindness to animals does not replace this specific category. |
| Courts of justice | 9:14 | Explain the role of lawful human institutions. An AI has no authority to punish or enforce. |

## One question, one source, one useful step

Choose a category connected to a real question. Open the source and name the edition. Separate its words from the explanation. Write down what remains uncertain and bring the question to a qualified rav. After receiving guidance, choose one manageable action and review whether it helped. The measure is a person's conduct, not whether the AI produced a declaration of agreement.

The AI should ask only for the information needed to help. It should not collect private confessions, judge another person's ultimate standing, or pressure someone into a declaration of faith.

## Keep distinctions visible

[Hilchot Melachim 8:11](https://www.sefaria.org/Mishneh_Torah,_Kings_and_Wars.8.11) describes the pious of the nations with a stated condition: accepting and observing the laws because Hashem commanded them in the Torah and informed us through Moshe of the earlier command to Noach's descendants. Its final words have the readings ולא מחכמיהם and אלא מחכמיהם; compare the [Mechon Mamre text](https://mechon-mamre.org/i/e508.htm). That variant does not remove the preceding condition.

[Chapter 10](https://www.sefaria.org/Mishneh_Torah,_Kings_and_Wars.10) makes further distinctions about Torah study and voluntary mitzvot. A qualified rav should help choose the appropriate path. Jewish and Noahide obligations should not be treated as interchangeable, including in questions about coercion and sanctifying the Name (10:2).

If life is immediately threatened, seek emergency human help. Do not wait for an AI to finish a study exercise.
